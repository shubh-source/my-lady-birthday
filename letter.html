A LITTLE NOTE FROM ME TO YOU

Dear My Lady,

✳

Happy birthday to the girl who feels like sunshine, laughter, and home all at once. Today is all about celebrating the most extraordinary soul in the universe — My Lady.

I wanted to take this special moment to tell you how truly blessed and lucky I am to have you as my best friend. You aren’t just my closest confidante; you are my anchor, my loudest cheerleader, my partner in crime, and my soul sister. Through every phase of life, every wild story, and every emotional breakdown, you’ve held my hand and had my back without a second thought.

Even though miles and distance separate us right now, My Lady, no matter how much the distance between us, our bond will remain the same always. Distance is just a tiny number when two hearts are as deeply tied as ours. No matter how many cities lie between us, one phone call or one text from you makes it feel like you are sitting right next to me, holding my hand and laughing at our dumb inside jokes.

Thank you for listening to all my endless drama across the phone, for wiping my tears from afar, and for always being my safest haven. No one in this world understands me the way My Lady does, and no distance could ever change that.

I hope this new year brings you the same boundless kindness, peace, and immense happiness you effortlessly bring into my life every single day. Keep dreaming fearlessly and slaying with that royal grace, because you deserve the entire universe and so much more. I can't wait until we reunite and give you the biggest, tightest hug!

With all my love across the miles and my whole heart,
Your Best Friend & Soul Sister ♡
